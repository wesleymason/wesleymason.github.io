Privacy

Privacy policy

Tab keeps your data on your iPhone. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Effective November 10, 2026

What stays on your device

Bills, tips, splits, receipts, receipt photos, notes and the cards you add to your wallet are stored in Tab's own storage on your iPhone. They are included in your iPhone backups like any app's data. Tab never sends them to us or to anyone else.

Camera and Photos

Tab uses the camera to scan and photograph receipts. Reading the amounts off a receipt happens on your iPhone. With your permission, Tab adds the photos you take to your Photos library; it only adds photos and never reads your library. Photos you choose from your library are shown to Tab only when you pick them.

Apple Watch

The Tab app for Apple Watch runs on its own. Nothing leaves your devices.

Card suggestions

The card advisor downloads a public list of credit card dining rewards from GitHub so it can stay current. That request carries your IP address, as any download does, and nothing else: no identifier, no bills and no cards of yours. GitHub's privacy statement covers that request.

Sharing a split

When you share a split, the amounts travel inside the link itself, in the part after the #, which browsers never send to a server. Nothing is stored on a server. Where the link goes is up to you and the app you send it with.

Purchases

Tab Pro is bought through the App Store. Apple handles the payment, and Tab never sees your payment details.

Children

Tab is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted here with its effective date.
